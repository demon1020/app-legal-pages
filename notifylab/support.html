Getting started

  1. Open NotifyLab and add a project or business.
  2. Import a Firebase service account JSON file for a project you are authorized to use.
  3. Open the project and configure your notification target and message.
  4. Review the target and content, then send from the developer workspace.

Common questions

Do I need a NotifyLab account?

No. NotifyLab's current core workflow does not require you to create a NotifyLab account.

Why might a notification fail?

Check that the service account is valid, the Firebase project is correct, required messaging permissions and APIs are configured, and the target token or topic is valid. Delivery also depends on Firebase and the receiving app.

How do I delete my local data?

Remove projects and history using any deletion controls provided in your installed version. On Android, you can also clear the App's storage in system settings; uninstalling may remove local data, subject to device backups. For help, email support. Removing local data does not automatically delete information previously sent to Firebase or recipients.

How do I revoke imported credentials?

Revoke or delete the relevant service account key in your Google Cloud/Firebase project and remove the local project configuration from NotifyLab. Deleting the local copy alone does not invalidate the key.

Does NotifyLab guarantee delivery?

No. Firebase delivery depends on project configuration, target validity, network availability, device state, and Google's infrastructure.

How do I get help with ads or Pro?

Email support with your app version and a description of the issue. For actual purchases, include only non-sensitive order information; never send payment card details.
