NotifyLab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026 · Published by Baburam Nabik under the BAITECH brand

NotifyLab is developed and published by Baburam Nabik under the BAITECH brand. Privacy inquiries may be sent to contact@baitech.in. This Privacy Policy explains how NotifyLab (the “App”) handles information when you configure Firebase projects and send Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) notifications. NotifyLab is designed as a local-first developer tool and does not require a NotifyLab account to use its core functionality.

1. Information you provide

Depending on how you use the App, you may provide project or business names, Firebase service account JSON credentials, Firebase project identifiers, recipient tokens or topics, notification titles and messages, and related configuration. You are responsible for having authorization to use the Firebase projects, credentials, and notification recipients you configure.

2. Local storage

NotifyLab is designed to keep project configuration, imported credentials, preferences, and recent notification-send history on your device. Sensitive credentials are intended to be stored using platform-provided secure storage where supported. Storage protections may differ by platform, device configuration, and browser. Storage behavior may differ by platform and browser.

3. Firebase authentication and notification delivery

To send notifications, the App must communicate with Google Firebase services. This may involve transmitting authentication information, access tokens, Firebase project identifiers, target identifiers, and notification content to Google. The exact authentication flow depends on the App implementation. Do not assume that all information stays on your device simply because NotifyLab has no proprietary backend. Google's processing is governed by its applicable terms and privacy documentation.

4. Advertising and third-party services

When ads are enabled, advertising providers may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics depending on SDK settings and user choices. See the privacy notices of the advertising providers identified in the app and applicable consent interfaces. Advertising and analytics practices must be reflected in the Google Play Data safety disclosure.

5. Pro features and payments

NotifyLab may offer optional Pro features. Purchase and transaction data, if paid purchases are enabled, may be processed by the applicable app store or payment provider. Purchase terms are shown before checkout. A development-only local unlock is not a completed purchase.

6. Purposes of processing

Information is used to configure Firebase projects, authenticate authorized requests, deliver notifications, display recent activity, remember app preferences, support paid features where applicable, and display advertising where enabled.

7. Sharing and disclosures

NotifyLab may transmit information to Google Firebase to authenticate and deliver notifications and to advertising or billing providers when their features are enabled. We do not represent these transmissions as a sale of user data.

8. Retention and deletion

Project data, imported credentials, and send history are intended to remain locally until removed through available App controls or app-data deletion. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may remove local information, subject to operating-system backups and platform behavior. Information already transmitted to Firebase, notification recipients, ad providers, or billing providers is subject to those parties' retention practices. For help deleting locally stored data, contact contact@baitech.in.

9. Security

We aim to protect locally stored credentials through appropriate platform storage mechanisms. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Service account keys can grant substantial access to Firebase resources; safeguard them, limit permissions, and revoke compromised keys promptly.

10. Children's privacy

NotifyLab is designed for developer and business use and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to BAITECH, contact contact@baitech.in.

11. International processing

Third-party services, including Google services, may process data in countries other than your country of residence, subject to their policies and applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. The current version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

Your privacy rights and grievance contact

Subject to applicable Indian law, including provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rules, 2025 as they become effective, you may contact Baburam Nabik (BAITECH) to request information about personal data processed under our control, correction or erasure where applicable, or to raise a grievance. You may also exercise any applicable rights to withdraw consent or nominate another person as permitted by law. Email contact@baitech.in with the subject “NotifyLab Privacy Request”. We will review and respond in accordance with applicable legal timelines. Requests relating to data independently controlled by Google, Firebase project owners, or other providers may need to be directed to those parties.

Security incidents and legal obligations

We assess suspected personal-data incidents and will provide notices to affected individuals and authorities when required by applicable law. We retain information under our control only for purposes and periods justified by its use and legal obligations; device-local information remains subject to device settings, backups, and user deletion actions.
